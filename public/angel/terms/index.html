Friend Beta Terms

Updated August 15, 2026

AngelAI is an experimental beta product.

These terms are written for the current friend beta and may be replaced by final production terms before a wider release.

Using the beta

By using the AngelAI friend beta, you agree to use it lawfully and in a way that does not intentionally harm other people, compromise accounts or systems, or misuse private information.

Who the current beta is for

The current friend beta is intended for adults unless EdenCore explicitly authorizes a separate youth experience. A future youth version requires its own age-appropriate safety, consent, privacy, and guardian design.

What AngelAI is

AngelAI is a personal AI companion and general-purpose assistant that can use conversation context, saved memories, user-provided sources, and other enabled features to personalize responses. Features may be experimental, incomplete, unavailable, or changed during beta.

No guarantee of accuracy

AI responses can be incomplete, mistaken, outdated, or unsuitable for a particular situation. Users should verify important information before acting on it, especially for medical, legal, financial, safety, or other high-stakes decisions.

Not an emergency or professional service

AngelAI is not an emergency service and is not a substitute for licensed medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or other professional care. If immediate safety is at risk, use appropriate local emergency or professional services.

Your content

You are responsible for the information and materials you choose to provide to AngelAI, including Library sources. Do not upload material you do not have the right to use. The current product is designed so private user content remains user-scoped and is not exposed through the founder beta-report inbox.

Beta changes and availability

EdenCore may change, limit, suspend, or remove beta features while testing safety, reliability, privacy, and product quality. Beta availability is not guaranteed.

Account access

Keep your account credentials secure. You are responsible for activity performed through your account unless applicable law provides otherwise.

Feedback

Optional bug reports and product feedback may be used to improve AngelAI. The current beta reporter is intentionally designed not to automatically attach conversation text, Library passages, screenshots, credentials, or reporter identity to the founder review view.
