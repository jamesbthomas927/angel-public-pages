Support

Friend beta · Updated August 15, 2026

Something feel wrong?

The fastest beta-support path is the in-app reporter. It is designed to send a short, privacy-bounded report to the founder review inbox.

Report an issue in AngelAI

Open You / Settings in AngelAI and use the beta-report section. Choose the closest category and briefly explain what happened.

  • Bug — something did not work correctly.
  • Angel misunderstood me — the response missed your meaning.
  • Memory is wrong — Angel remembered or interpreted something incorrectly.
  • Source problem — a Library source, citation, or grounding answer appears unsupported.
  • Idea — a product suggestion or improvement.

What a beta report includes

The current report path is limited to your chosen category, your short description, the screen, platform, app version when available, status, and timestamps. It does not automatically attach chats, Library text, screenshots, credentials, or account identity to the founder review view.

Account and data issues

For account deletion, memory correction, Library source removal, or other privacy controls, use the controls available inside AngelAI. If a required control is unavailable during beta, report it through the beta reporter as a bug or privacy issue.

Urgent safety

AngelAI is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact appropriate local emergency services or a trusted person who can provide immediate real-world help.

Beta limitations

This support page covers the friend beta. Response times are not guaranteed, and features may change while AngelAI is being tested.
